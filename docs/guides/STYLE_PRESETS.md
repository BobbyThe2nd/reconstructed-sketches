# Style Presets

## 1. Quiet impossible study

- **Perspective:** two local vanishing systems, one extra-face reveal.
- **Line:** graphite contour, faint redrawn corners, long construction rays.
- **Color:** graphite on warm off-white.
- **Best for:** small objects, lamps, tools, modest buildings.

## 2. Folded architectural memory

- **Perspective:** split horizon, compressed facade bay, expanded distant roof plane.
- **Line:** careful structural outlines with pale alignment lines.
- **Color:** gray-blue, faded brick, charcoal.
- **Best for:** houses, storefronts, industrial sheds.

## 3. Workshop inversion

- **Perspective:** locally rotated attachment, near-small / far-large repeated part.
- **Line:** slightly heavier mechanical contour, visible correction marks.
- **Color:** dusty blue, ochre, warm brown.
- **Best for:** vehicles, machines, furniture.

## 4. Colored-pencil model

- **Perspective:** three compatible-but-not-identical plane systems, thin exposed underside.
- **Line:** colored contour with graphite construction beneath.
- **Color:** sage, muted red, charcoal on cream paper.
- **Best for:** designed objects and domestic scenes.

## Selection rule

Choose one preset as the base. Borrow at most one feature from another preset so the output remains a coherent drawing rather than a collage of effects.
